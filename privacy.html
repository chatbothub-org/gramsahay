Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains how GramSahay, an independent WhatsApp-based citizen communication service, handles information when you use our website, WhatsApp demo, or an area where the platform has been implemented.

Important: GramSahay is not a government organization and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government. We do not directly solve complaints or guarantee results. A real local request is routed to a participating local leader or organization only where GramSahay has been implemented. A demo ticket is for testing and is not a government complaint.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use and the details you choose to provide, we may collect:

  • Your name and WhatsApp/mobile number.
  • Village, Gram Panchayat, ward, locality or other area details.
  • Issue categories, descriptions, messages, photographs, attachments and details about the person for whom a request is submitted.
  • Ticket IDs, assigned team details, request status, comments and status history.
  • Demo interactions, feedback, communication preferences and basic technical or usage information needed to operate or secure the service.

Please avoid sending Aadhaar numbers, bank account details, passwords, OTPs, or other sensitive documents unless specifically necessary and requested through a trusted process. Do not submit another person's information unless you have an appropriate basis to do so.

2. How We Use Information

  • To register demo or active-area requests, create tickets, route eligible issues and show status updates.
  • To enable participating leaders, local organizations and authorized team members to follow up on requests.
  • To respond to support questions, maintain service records, prevent abuse and improve reliability.
  • To prepare aggregated or de-identified reports where appropriate.
  • To send service-related WhatsApp updates and, where applicable and permitted, separately opted-in greetings, announcements or other messages.

Submitting a request does not guarantee that any authority or organization will resolve it. Test tickets may be closed without action on a real-world issue.

3. Who May Receive Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. Relevant information may be shared with:

  • Participating local leaders, organizations and authorized support teams in an active GramSahay area, to handle the submitted issue.
  • Technology and hosting providers that help deliver the service, including WhatsApp/Meta and infrastructure providers, as applicable.
  • Authorities when disclosure is legally required or otherwise permitted by applicable law.

Government submission is not automatic: GramSahay does not automatically lodge your complaint with Odisha Jana Sunani or another government portal. If you choose to use a government service, its separate privacy practices apply.

4. WhatsApp Messages and Your Choices

Our WhatsApp service uses WhatsApp Business tools. WhatsApp's own terms and privacy notice also apply: WhatsApp Privacy Policy.

We may send messages necessary for a request or demo, such as ticket acknowledgements and updates. Optional promotional messages, festival greetings or campaigns should be sent only where an appropriate legal basis and required WhatsApp permission exist. You can ask us to stop optional messages or withdraw consent by contacting customer care. We will explain any further steps required to process your request.

5. Data Retention

We keep records only for as long as reasonably needed for request follow-up, support, security, legitimate recordkeeping and applicable legal obligations. Retention may differ for demo tickets, live requests and support messages. We will review deletion requests and explain where information must be retained for legal or operational reasons.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards intended to limit unauthorized access, misuse, loss and disclosure. Access should be limited to people who need the information to operate or support the service. No online service can promise absolute security.

7. Your Privacy Requests

Subject to applicable law, you may ask what personal information we hold, request correction or deletion, withdraw consent where consent applies, raise a grievance, or ask how your complaint data is handled. Contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

8. Children and Requests on Behalf of Others

GramSahay is intended primarily for adults. If a request concerns a child (a person under 18 in India), a parent or lawful guardian should provide or authorize the relevant information as required by applicable law. Do not share children's personal information unnecessarily. Contact us if a child's information has been submitted inappropriately.

9. Independent Service and Demo Limitations

GramSahay provides technology to connect citizens and local support teams. It is not an emergency response service, government grievance portal or public authority. An area must have a participating leader or organization for local routing to operate. For actual Odisha Government grievances, you may separately use Odisha Jana Sunani.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this policy when our services or legal obligations change. The date above will indicate the latest version. When required, we will provide additional notice and seek any necessary consent; continued use alone does not replace consent required by law.
