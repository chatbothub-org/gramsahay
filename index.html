The citizen journey

From request to resolution—in eight clear steps

The location fields adapt to each deployment—Gram Panchayat and village in rural areas, or Ward, sector and Nagar in cities.

STEP 01👋

Send “Hi”

The citizen starts a WhatsApp conversation and enters their name.

STEP 02👤

Choose self or others

A request can be raised for oneself or on behalf of someone who needs help.

STEP 03📍

Select area or locality

Choose the village, sector, Nagar or other configured local area.

STEP 04☰

Select Ward or GP

The citizen selects the relevant Ward, Gram Panchayat or local administrative unit.

STEP 05✍️

Choose and describe issue

Select the issue category and add a short description for the local team.

STEP 06🎫

Ticket is created

A unique ID and request summary are sent to the citizen automatically.

STEP 07🔔

Local team is notified

The relevant village, Ward, sector or Nagar team receives the request.

STEP 08✅

Update and close

The team updates the status, closes the request and keeps the citizen informed.

GramSahay is not only a complaint inbox—it connects the citizen to a local helper, creates a ticket and supports follow-up until a clear outcome.
